# ICA Media Portal - Design and product brief

Design read: A trusted, independent Iraqi-Chinese media and cultural journalism portal for readers, reporters, institutions and bilateral communities.
Concept spine: The red editorial ribbon connects two places and many voices; images, reports and archives travel along the same visual current.
Delivery tier: cinema for homepage film; editorial for article reading and newsroom workflows.
Palette: ICA cherry #E3001B, burgundy #361019, ivory #FCFBF8, charcoal #242126, crisp white #FFFFFF. An established user-selected ICA brand.
Type: Geist-style geometric headlines and sans body (local/system stack); editorial seriousness without ornamental serif.
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot
Journey: 1) The bridge, a red ribbon linking cultural objects; headline "Stories across borders"; 2) The lens of reporting, reporter camera reveals details; headline "Reporting with context"; 3) The archive, human documents and culture connected in one material composition; headline "A shared conversation".
World grammar: editorial documentary photograph, one studio table with Kurdish copper and Chinese porcelain, cherry-red silk ribbon, warm right-side light, burgundy negative space, physically credible objects.
Mobile framing: center-safe ribbon and camera; mobile encode with content fit and original first-frame poster.
Journey purpose: A continuous red ribbon connects reporting, culture and video before readers enter the practical newsroom.
Section plan, homepage: (1) editorial-film hero, (2) issue lead rail, (3) horizontal newsroom feed, (4) image-led cultural split, (5) video stage panorama, (6) asymmetric newsletter strip, (7) concise footer directory.
Visual assets: original editorial film, original photography, culture image, cinema still, ruby ribbon icon family, ICA monogram, OG launch cover, full favicon and PWA set.
CTA inventory: hero "Read news" framed card; newsroom "Browse coverage" underlined editorial link; video "Watch library" large poster target; newsletter "Subscribe" bordered submit; editorial "Submit story" outline action.
Languages: English, Arabic, Kurdish Sorani and Simplified Chinese; interface translated, editorial content only in reviewed published languages.
Trust rules: never invent live news, partner endorsement, press credentials or a signed-by reporter; draft and sample articles are never public.
Architecture: Cloudflare Worker + D1 + R2; public routes + protected editorial studio with signed 12-hour cookie; editor bootstrap key configured in site secrets; no unsafe public admin enrollment.
Security: validated fields, parameterized SQL, private editor sessions, uploads restricted, no public draft leak, strict file size and URL checks.
Publishing policy: only reviewed items marked published appear in public channels; no preloaded fictional news.
SEO: route titles and canonical links, sitemap and robots; brand-safe OG metadata.
Responsive: six-step fluid scale, 2-column cards on desktop and single-column mobile, accessible keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support.
Production readiness: all public modules and backend must deploy, editorial login requires configured secret, honest empty states until authentic content arrives.
Community feed: not requested; deploy live without listing.
Design rationale: newsroom-grade restraint and ICA cherry accent rather than decorative startup graphics.
